## FAQ Guide

### What is the difference between BK025 and BK020?

BK025 steps the carbon black up to 25% (from BK020's 20%) and drops the CaCO₃ filler to 37.8% (from 47.8%). The result is a noticeably deeper black with less filler, while staying cost-friendly. If BK020 looks slightly weak or grey in your film, BK025 is the natural next step up.

### What is the difference between BK025 and BK030?

BK030 raises carbon black to 30% and cuts filler to 27.8%, giving an even richer black for applications that need more depth. BK025 keeps a bit more filler, so it costs less per kilogram. Choose BK025 when cost matters more than maximum blackness, and BK030 when you want a balanced mid-range grade.

### Is BK025 suitable for blown film?

Yes. BK025 is formulated for film blowing, with a matched LLDPE + LDPE carrier, stable dispersion, and 0.2% max moisture. Its 25% loading gives good opacity and deep black for packaging and shopping bags. If you need the deepest black or the lowest dosage, consider BK035 or BK040 instead.

### Is BK025 suitable for injection molding?

Yes. BK025 works well for injection molding where you want more depth than an economy grade. Dispersion is clean with no carbon black spots, and its MFI (3–50 g/10 min) can be adjusted to match your molding machine. Recommended addition is 2%–5%.

### How much filler does BK025 contain, and what does it mean?

BK025 contains 37.8% CaCO₃ filler, which lowers the cost per kilogram while keeping a consistent black. The trade-off is slightly lower gloss and strength versus a low-filler grade. If you need higher gloss or a cleaner black, our BK035 (17.8%) or BK040 (5.8%) carry less filler.

### What is the recommended addition ratio for BK025?

We recommend 2%–5% by weight, depending on your product thickness and target blackness. Start with a small trial on your own line to find your exact ratio, then lock it in for repeat runs.

### What carrier resin does BK025 use, and why does it matter?

BK025 uses a 25% LLDPE + LDPE carrier, matched to polyethylene film and molding. A matched carrier means the pellet melts and mixes cleanly inside your PE process window, with no weak spots or delamination. If you process PP instead of PE, tell us and we will recommend a PP carrier grade.

### Can I get a free sample of BK025?

Yes. We provide a free sample of BK025 so you can check blackness, dispersion, and processing on your own line before ordering. Send us your base resin and application, and we will arrange a trial sample.
