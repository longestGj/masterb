## FAQ Guide

### What is the difference between BK035 and BK030?

BK035 steps carbon black up to 35% (from BK030's 30%) and cuts CaCO₃ filler to 17.8% (from 27.8%). You get a deeper, richer black with less filler and a lower addition rate. If BK030 is not quite deep enough for your film or parts, BK035 is the next step up.

### What is the difference between BK035 and BK040?

BK040 is our top grade, at 40% carbon black and just 5.8% filler, for maximum jetness and the lowest dosage. BK035 keeps slightly more filler, so it costs a bit less per kilogram while still delivering premium black. Choose BK035 for high blackness at a friendlier price, and BK040 when you want the maximum.

### Is BK035 suitable for trash bags?

Yes — BK035 is formulated mainly for trash bags. Its 35% loading gives very strong opacity and deep black in thin film, with a lower addition rate than mid-range grades. The low 17.8% filler keeps gloss and film clarity high. If you need the absolute deepest black, consider BK040.

### Is BK035 suitable for injection molding?

Yes. BK035 is also made for injection molding, with clean dispersion, no carbon black spots, and an adjustable MFI (3–50 g/10 min) to match your machine. The higher loading gives rich, uniform black on molded parts at a lower dose.

### How much filler does BK035 contain, and what does it mean?

BK035 contains only 17.8% CaCO₃ filler — much lower than our economy grades. Less filler means higher gloss, cleaner black, and better mechanical properties, at a slightly higher cost per kilogram. The high loading offsets that by letting you dose less, so your cost per ton of finished product stays competitive.

### What is the recommended addition ratio for BK035?

We recommend 2%–5% by weight, but the high loading often lets you run toward the lower end. Start with a small trial on your own line to find your exact ratio and confirm the blackness, then lock it in for repeat runs.

### What carrier resin does BK035 use, and why does it matter?

BK035 uses a 37% LLDPE + LDPE carrier, matched to polyethylene trash-bag film and molding. A matched carrier means the pellet melts and mixes cleanly in your PE process window with no weak spots. If you process PP instead of PE, tell us and we will recommend a PP carrier grade.

### Can I get a free sample of BK035?

Yes. We provide a free sample of BK035 so you can check blackness, dispersion, and processing on your own line before ordering. Send us your base resin and application, and we will arrange a trial sample.
