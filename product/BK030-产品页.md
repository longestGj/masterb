## FAQ Guide

### What is the difference between BK030 and BK020?

BK030 steps carbon black up to 30% (from BK020's 20%) and drops CaCO₃ filler to 27.8% (from 47.8%). You get a noticeably deeper, richer black with less filler, while still keeping a cost-friendly price. If BK020 looks grey or thin in your trash bags, BK030 is the step up most customers choose.

### What is the difference between BK030 and BK035?

BK035 raises carbon black to 35% and cuts filler to 17.8%, giving an even deeper black and a lower addition rate for demanding parts. BK030 keeps more filler, so it costs less per kilogram. Choose BK030 for balanced cost and blackness, and BK035 when you need maximum depth at a lower dosage.

### Is BK030 suitable for trash bags?

Yes — BK030 is formulated mainly for trash bags. Its 30% loading gives strong opacity and deep black in thin film, and its matched LLDPE + LDPE carrier with stable dispersion keeps defects out of the gauge. If you need the deepest black or the lowest dosage, consider BK035 or BK040.

### Is BK030 suitable for injection molding?

Yes. BK030 is also made for injection molding, with clean dispersion, no carbon black spots, and an adjustable MFI (3–50 g/10 min) to match your machine. It gives uniform color and good surface finish on general black molded parts.

### How much filler does BK030 contain, and what does it mean?

BK030 contains 27.8% CaCO₃ filler, a middle level that lowers cost per kilogram while keeping a consistent black. Less filler than BK020 or BK025 means richer black; more than BK035 or BK040 means a lower price. It is the balanced option in our range.

### What is the recommended addition ratio for BK030?

We recommend 2%–5% by weight, depending on product thickness and target blackness. Start with a small trial on your own line to find your exact ratio, then lock it in for repeat runs.

### What carrier resin does BK030 use, and why does it matter?

BK030 uses a 32% LLDPE + LDPE carrier, matched to polyethylene trash-bag film and molding. A matched carrier means the pellet melts and mixes cleanly in your PE process window with no weak spots. If you process PP instead of PE, tell us and we will recommend a PP carrier grade.

### Can I get a free sample of BK030?

Yes. We provide a free sample of BK030 so you can check blackness, dispersion, and processing on your own line before ordering. Send us your base resin and application, and we will arrange a trial sample.
