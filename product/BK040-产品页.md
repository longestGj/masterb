## FAQ Guide

### What is the difference between BK040 and BK035?

BK040 is our top grade at 40% carbon black and just 5.8% filler, versus BK035's 35% and 17.8%. You get the deepest black, highest gloss, and the lowest addition rate. Choose BK040 when maximum jetness matters, and BK035 when you want premium black at a slightly friendlier price.

### Is BK040 our highest carbon black grade?

Yes. BK040 is the top of our range at 40% virgin carbon black. Going much beyond 40% in a PE carrier makes dispersion harder and the pellet dustier to feed, so 40% is the practical premium point for most film and molding work. If you have a special high-loading requirement, tell us and we can discuss a custom grade.

### Is BK040 suitable for blown film?

Yes — BK040 is formulated for film blowing. Its 40% loading gives maximum opacity and deep black in thin gauge, with the lowest addition rate in our range. The near-filler-free formula keeps film gloss and clarity high. It is our top choice for premium black film.

### Is BK040 suitable for injection molding?

Yes. BK040 is also made for injection molding, with clean dispersion, no carbon black spots, and an adjustable MFI (3–50 g/10 min) to match your machine. The high loading gives rich, uniform, high-gloss black on molded parts at a low dose.

### How much filler does BK040 contain, and what does it mean?

BK040 contains only 5.8% CaCO₃ filler — the lowest in our range. That means maximum gloss, cleanest black, and the best mechanical properties, since nearly all the pellet is pigment and carrier. The slightly higher cost per kilogram is offset by the low addition rate, so your cost per ton of finished product stays competitive.

### What is the recommended addition ratio for BK040?

We recommend 2%–5% by weight, but the high loading usually lets you run toward the lower end — often 1.5%–2.5% — to reach a deep black. Start with a small trial on your own line to find your exact ratio, then lock it in for repeat runs.

### What carrier resin does BK040 use, and why does it matter?

BK040 uses a 40% LLDPE + LDPE carrier, matched to polyethylene film and molding. A matched carrier means the pellet melts and mixes cleanly in your PE process window with no weak spots. If you process PP instead of PE, tell us and we will recommend a PP carrier grade.

### Can I get a free sample of BK040?

Yes. We provide a free sample of BK040 so you can check jetness, gloss, dispersion, and processing on your own line before ordering. Send us your base resin and application, and we will arrange a trial sample.
