How your RFQ is reviewed

  1. Share your resin, application, process and target requirement.
  2. Describe any current issue, known grade and document need.
  3. The team reviews product and document scope by exact grade and application.
  4. We reply by email with the next suitable step.

Document availability depends on exact product grade, application and review.
