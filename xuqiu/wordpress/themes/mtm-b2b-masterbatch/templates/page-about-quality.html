What happens after you send an inquiry.

The team checks the application, possible product direction, missing information and document scope before replying in writing.

  1. 01Share the production context

    Resin, process, end product, target, current grade and document needs.

    Buyer input: known grade or sample reference, quantity and target market.
  2. 02Screen the application and possible product direction

    Review the application, resin, process and known grade information without promising a final grade.

    Review: resin family, process window, current issue and end-use requirement.
  3. 03Check missing information and document scope

    Identify what still needs clarification and confirm requested documents against the product and intended use.

    Clarify: missing inputs, product identity, destination and intended use.
  4. 04Reply with the next suitable step

    Explain whether the next step is more information, a sample discussion, document review or technical-commercial follow-up.

    Output: next action, open questions and document scope.

Document review.

Technical and compliance-related documents are not published for direct retrieval. Buyers submit a request, and the team reviews the exact grade, application, country and document scope before replying by email.

Compliance-related documents, if available, apply only to specific tested grades or samples.

Claim limits.

To keep claims accurate, these details remain unpublished until the business evidence is confirmed.

  1. Factory scale

    Production line count, annual capacity or factory scale.

  2. Market and clients

    Number of markets served, customer names or brand partnerships.

  3. Lab and certification

    Laboratory equipment or a general quality or certification guarantee.

  4. Contact and positioning

    Detailed street address, phone number or a manufacturer positioning.

  5. Commercial terms

    Fixed MOQ, price, lead time or full-series compliance.

Buyer checks.

A useful inquiry leaves both sides with a written basis for the next decision. These four checks keep the discussion specific.

  1. Product identityConfirm the exact grade

    Match the product code, current grade or sample reference before treating products as equivalent.

  2. Document versionCheck the relevant file

    Confirm the document type, version date and the tested grade or sample it applies to.

  3. Application fitConnect use to the evidence

    Review resin, process, end product, destination and intended use against the available information.

  4. Written next stepAgree what happens next

    Record open questions, document scope, sample discussion or the next technical review before commercial terms.
