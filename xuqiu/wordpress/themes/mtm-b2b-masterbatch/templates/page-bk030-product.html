BK030 buyer FAQ

Points buyers ask before comparing BK030 with BK025 or BK035 and running a trial sample.

What is the difference between BK030 and BK020?

BK030 steps carbon black up to 30% from BK020's 20% and cuts CaCO₃ filler to 27.8% from 47.8%. The result is a noticeably deeper, richer black with less filler. If BK020 looks grey or thin in your trash bags, BK030 is the step up most buyers choose.

What is the difference between BK030 and BK035?

BK035 raises carbon black to 35% and cuts filler to 17.8% for a deeper black and a lower addition rate. BK030 keeps more filler and stays the balanced option in the range. Choose BK030 for balanced cost and blackness, and BK035 when you need more depth at a lower dosage.

Is BK030 suitable for trash bags?

BK030 is formulated mainly for trash bags. The 30% loading gives strong opacity and deep black in thin film, and the matched LLDPE + LDPE carrier with stable dispersion keeps defects out of the gauge. For the deepest black or the lowest dosage, compare BK035 or BK040.

Is BK030 suitable for injection molding?

Yes. BK030 is also positioned for injection molding with good dispersion, no carbon black specks and an adjustable MFI from 3–50 g/10 min at 190°C and 21.6kg.

How much filler does BK030 contain?

BK030 contains 27.8% CaCO₃ filler, a middle level in the range. Less filler than BK020 or BK025 means a richer black; more than BK035 or BK040 keeps it the balanced option. Compare the result at the actual addition rate rather than price per kilogram alone.

What addition ratio is recommended for BK030?

The TDS recommends 2%–5% by weight, adjustable to the production setup. A trial should establish the final ratio for the specific resin, process and blackness target.

What carrier resin does BK030 use?

BK030 uses a 32% LLDPE + LDPE carrier, matched to polyethylene trash bag film and molding. If the production resin is PP instead of PE, ask about a PP carrier grade and confirm compatibility.

Can I request a free sample of BK030?

Yes. Share the base resin and application so a trial sample can be arranged. Check blackness, dispersion and processing on your own line before ordering.
