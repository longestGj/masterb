BK035 buyer FAQ

Points buyers ask before comparing BK035 with BK030 or BK040 and running a trial sample.

What is the difference between BK035 and BK030?

BK035 steps carbon black up to 35% from BK030's 30% and cuts CaCO₃ filler to 17.8% from 27.8%. The result is a deeper, richer black with less filler and a lower addition rate. If BK030 is not quite deep enough for your film or parts, BK035 is the next step up.

What is the difference between BK035 and BK040?

BK040 is the top grade at 40% carbon black and 5.8% filler, for maximum jetness and the lowest dosage. BK035 keeps slightly more filler while still delivering premium black. Choose BK035 for high blackness one step below the top grade, and BK040 when you want the maximum.

Is BK035 suitable for trash bags?

BK035 is formulated mainly for trash bags. The 35% loading gives strong opacity and deep black in thin film with a lower addition rate than mid-range grades. The low 17.8% filler helps maintain gloss. Confirm performance in a trial on the actual film line.

Is BK035 suitable for injection molding?

Yes. BK035 is also positioned for injection molding with good dispersion, no carbon black specks and an adjustable MFI from 3–50 g/10 min at 190°C and 21.6kg.

How much filler does BK035 contain?

BK035 contains 17.8% CaCO₃ filler, well below the economy grades. Lower filler supports gloss, a cleaner black and mechanical properties. Compare the result at the actual addition rate rather than price per kilogram alone.

What addition ratio is recommended for BK035?

The TDS recommends 2%–5% by weight, adjustable to the production setup. The higher loading often lets you run toward the lower end. A trial should establish the final ratio for the specific resin, process and blackness target.

What carrier resin does BK035 use?

BK035 uses a 37% LLDPE + LDPE carrier, matched to polyethylene trash bag film and molding. If the production resin is PP instead of PE, ask about a PP carrier grade and confirm compatibility.

Can I request a free sample of BK035?

Yes. Share the base resin and application so a trial sample can be arranged. Check jetness, gloss, dispersion and processing on your own line before ordering.
