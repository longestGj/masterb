GE CHEMICAL & POLYMER GROUP CO., LTD reviews color masterbatch inquiries case by case after RFQ submission.

## What color masterbatch buyers usually need to clarify

A color name is not enough to review a custom requirement. The team needs the resin, processing method, final product, color reference type and target expectations.

Common inquiry situations include:

- An injection molding buyer needs a custom color for molded parts.
- A packaging or plastic product buyer needs to discuss color consistency.
- A distributor needs color reference and document review for a customer.
- A buyer needs to compare a current grade or previous supplier grade.

This page is an RFQ guide. It is not a fixed color specification or public document page.

## Information to prepare before requesting color masterbatch

To help the team review your inquiry, prepare:

- Base resin, such as PE, PP, ABS, PET, PVC or another resin.
- Processing method, such as injection molding, film blowing or extrusion.
- Final product or application.
- Color reference type: physical sample, Pantone, RAL, photo, existing product, previous supplier grade, customer standard or not sure.
- Available color reference details.
- Target color or function.
- Tolerance expectation, if your customer has one.
- Product grade or model, if known.
- Current grade or supplier, if any.
- Trial quantity and estimated monthly quantity.
- Required technical or compliance-related documents.

## Application context for color masterbatch RFQs

Color masterbatch inquiries may involve injection molded parts, packaging products, plastic bags, household plastic products or distributor sourcing. The application helps the team review resin compatibility and processing context.

Color matching and sample discussion are case by case after RFQ review. This page does not promise an exact Delta E, Pantone or RAL match, a sample timing, fixed commercial terms or batch consistency.

## What to include in your RFQ

| RFQ item | What to provide |
|---|---|
| Product interest | Color masterbatch |
| Base resin | PE, PP, ABS, PET, PVC or other |
| Processing method | Injection molding, film blowing, extrusion or other |
| Color reference type | Physical sample, Pantone, RAL, photo, existing product, previous grade, customer standard or not sure |
| Color reference details | Code, photo note, sample description, existing product details or customer standard |
| Application | Injection part, packaging, bag, household product, distributor inquiry or other |
| Target expectation | Buyer requirement for review |
| Product grade or model | Current grade, replacement target or known GE grade, if available |
| Quantity | Trial quantity and estimated monthly quantity |
| Documents requested | TDS, SDS/MSDS, COA or other files |

The more complete the RFQ is, the easier it is for the team to review color feasibility, sample discussion needs and document scope.

## Requesting technical documents

Technical documents are requested by form and reviewed manually.

Please submit the product interest, resin, application, country or region, document purpose and required file types. The team reviews whether suitable documents are available for the exact inquiry scope, then replies by email.

Document availability depends on exact product grade and application. Compliance-related documents, if available, apply only to specific tested grades or samples.

You can provide a physical sample, Pantone code, RAL code, photo, existing product, previous supplier grade, customer standard or select not sure.
