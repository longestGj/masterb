GE CHEMICAL & POLYMER GROUP CO., LTD reviews each white masterbatch inquiry by product grade, application and document scope before confirming technical data or document availability.

## What white masterbatch buyers usually need to clarify

White masterbatch inquiries often involve a balance between whiteness, opacity, resin compatibility, processing method and document requirements.

Common inquiry situations include:

- A film or packaging buyer needs to describe an opacity and appearance target.
- An injection molding buyer needs to discuss white coverage for molded parts.
- A distributor needs product and document review before customer approval.
- A buyer needs to discuss FDA-related or food-contact-related documents for a specific grade and use.

## Information to prepare before requesting white masterbatch

To help the team review your inquiry, prepare:

- Base resin, such as PE, PP, LDPE, LLDPE, HDPE or another resin.
- Processing method, such as film blowing, injection molding or extrusion.
- Final product or application.
- Whiteness or appearance target.
- Opacity requirement.
- TiO2 requirement, if specified by your customer or application.
- Product grade or model, if known.
- Current grade or supplier, if any.
- Trial quantity and estimated monthly quantity.
- Destination country or region and destination port.
- Required technical or compliance-related documents.

## Application context for white masterbatch RFQs

White masterbatch inquiries may involve film, packaging, plastic bags, injection molded parts or distributor sourcing. These are RFQ contexts used to understand the buyer requirement.

TiO2 may be discussed as part of the buyer requirement. This page does not publish a fixed TiO2 content or position a general product as a specified TiO2 grade.

Suitability depends on the exact grade, resin, processing method, opacity target, whiteness target and document scope.

## What to include in your RFQ

| RFQ item | What to provide |
|---|---|
| Product interest | White masterbatch |
| Base resin | PE, PP, LDPE, LLDPE, HDPE or other |
| Processing method | Film blowing, injection molding, extrusion or other |
| Application | Film, packaging, bag, injection part, distributor inquiry or other |
| Target requirement | Whiteness, opacity, appearance or TiO2 context |
| Product grade or model | Current grade, replacement target or known GE grade, if available |
| Quantity | Trial quantity and estimated monthly quantity |
| Destination | Country or region and destination port |
| Documents requested | TDS, SDS/MSDS, COA, FDA-related, food-contact-related or other files |

The more complete the RFQ is, the easier it is for the team to review product fit and document scope.

## Requesting technical and compliance-related documents

Technical and compliance-related documents are requested by form and reviewed manually.

Please submit the product interest, application, country or region, document purpose and required file types. The team reviews whether suitable documents are available for the exact grade and application, then replies by email.

FDA-related or food-contact-related documents, if available, apply only to specific tested grades or samples.

## Evidence and claim boundaries

- FDA-related evidence, if discussed, applies only to the specific tested GE Chemical white sample or grade identified in the approved file.
- Final data should be confirmed by exact GE Chemical grade.
- TiO2 content, opacity, whiteness and dosage are not fixed promises on this page.
- Documents are provided only after form request and manual review.
- This page does not claim that all white masterbatch is FDA compliant, food-contact suitable or compliant as a full product line.

## Frequently asked questions

### What information is needed before requesting white masterbatch?

Please provide resin, application, processing method, whiteness target, opacity requirement, TiO2 context if any, product grade or model if known, quantity and document needs.

### Can I request FDA-related or food-contact-related documents?

Yes. Request documents by form. The team reviews the exact grade, application, country and document purpose before replying by email.

### Does all white masterbatch meet food-contact requirements?

No full-series claim should be made. Food-contact-related information, if available, applies only to specific tested grades or samples.

### Can TiO2 content or opacity be confirmed from this page?

No. TiO2 requirements and opacity expectations are RFQ inputs. Final data depends on the exact grade and application review.

### Are technical documents provided automatically?

No. Technical documents are requested by form and reviewed manually.
