## What black masterbatch buyers usually need to clarify

Black masterbatch selection depends on more than color. Buyers often need to clarify blackness, dispersion, carrier resin, processing method and compatibility with the final plastic product.

Common inquiry situations include:

- A plastic film or bag factory needs better blackness or coverage.
- An injection molding company wants cleaner dispersion and fewer visible carbon black spots.
- A processor using recycled resin needs to describe color variation and processing conditions.
- A distributor or importer needs product and document review before discussing supply.

Recycled plastic may be included as an RFQ context in this stage. This page is not a separate recycled plastic application page.

## Information to prepare before requesting black masterbatch

To help the team review your inquiry, please prepare:

- Base resin, such as PE, PP, LDPE, LLDPE, HDPE or another resin.
- Processing method, such as film blowing, injection molding, extrusion or re-granulating.
- Final product or application.
- Required blackness or color coverage target.
- Current issue, such as poor dispersion, color inconsistency or visible carbon black spots.
- Product grade or model, if known.
- Current grade or current supplier, if any.
- Existing addition ratio, if known.
- Trial quantity and estimated monthly quantity.
- Destination country or region and destination port.
- Documents needed for review.

## Application context for black masterbatch RFQs

Black masterbatch inquiries may involve film, plastic bags, injection molded parts, extrusion, recycled resin processing or distributor sourcing. These are RFQ contexts used to understand the buyer requirement.

Suitability depends on the exact grade, resin, processing method and product target. This page does not claim pipe-grade, UV-grade, potable-water grade, food-contact suitability or standards compliance unless exact grade-specific evidence is reviewed and approved.

## What to include in your RFQ

Please include the following information in your RFQ form:

| RFQ item | What to provide |
| --- | --- |
| Product interest | Black masterbatch |
| Base resin | PE, PP, LDPE, LLDPE, HDPE or other |
| Processing method | Film blowing, injection molding, extrusion, re-granulating or other |
| Application | Film, bag, injection part, recycled resin context, distributor inquiry or other |
| Target requirement | Blackness, coverage, dispersion or current issue |
| Product grade or model | Current grade, target replacement model or known GE grade, if available |
| Quantity | Trial quantity and monthly quantity |
| Destination | Country or region and destination port |
| Documents requested | TDS, SDS/MSDS, COA, RoHS-related files or other technical files |

The more complete the RFQ is, the easier it is for the team to review product fit and document scope.
