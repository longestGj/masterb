<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Desiccant masterbatch is a functional concentrate for moisture that reaches a plastic process despite storage and drying controls. It is selected for moisture control, not pigment, opacity or color.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Water can turn into steam inside the barrel and appear as bubbles, voids, splay, gels or an unstable surface. A CaO desiccant acts during processing, while pre-drying, sealed storage and dry handling remain the first controls.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2>Where desiccant masterbatch fits</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Desiccant masterbatch is reviewed when moisture still reaches the melt through washed regrind, humid storage, outdoor resin handling or an open hopper. It does not replace a dryer, correct wet packaging or diagnose trapped air, degradation gas or poor venting.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul>
<li><strong>Injection molding:</strong> review splay, bubbles, voids and surface defects against the resin and storage history.</li>
<li><strong>Extrusion:</strong> check feedstock, moisture source, surface target and any interaction with the existing additive package.</li>
<li><strong>Film blowing:</strong> confirm bubbles, gels, gauge stability and appearance on a controlled PE film trial.</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<h2>What to prepare for a moisture-control review</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul>
<li>Base resin, regrind share and current grade or supplier.</li>
<li>Injection, extrusion or film-blowing process and finished product.</li>
<li>Moisture source, storage history and current defects.</li>
<li>Planned dosage, color tolerance and whether the recycled carrier is accepted.</li>
<li>Trial quantity, monthly volume and required TDS, SDS or declaration scope.</li>
</ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2>How a first trial is controlled</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Record the resin, storage condition and current defect before dosing DM2476G. Keep the vacuum packaging sealed until use, start near the TDS 1% level and compare the run against the same material without desiccant. Check bubbles, voids, splay, surface, any grey tint and processing stability before fixing the recipe.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Moisture-related bubbles are not the only possible cause. If the result does not change, review resin wetness, dryer performance, venting, temperature and contamination before increasing dosage.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2>Desiccant masterbatch buyer FAQ</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Does DM2476G replace pre-drying?</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>No. Dry storage and pre-drying remain first. DM2476G is evaluated when moisture still reaches the process after those controls.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Is the 1% dosage universal?</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>No. The current DM2476G TDS lists 1% as a recommended starting dosage. The production dose must be confirmed on the resin, process and finished product.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Can DM2476G be used with recycled PE?</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The grade itself uses a 24% recycled PE carrier and is listed for general injection, extrusion and film blowing. Confirm the recycled-content rules, the condition of the regrind and the required documents before approval.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3>Does Food Grade appear on the DM2476G TDS?</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>No. The current TDS does not list Food Grade. Do not infer FDA, EU or customer food-contact approval without the applicable documents.</p>
