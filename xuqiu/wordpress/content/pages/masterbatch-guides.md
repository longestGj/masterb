The product pages and buyer resources answer different questions. Start with the material closest to the application, then use the inquiry and document guidance to prepare a reviewable request.

## Choose a product direction

Review the [Black masterbatch](/products/black-masterbatch/),
[White masterbatch](/products/white-masterbatch/) and
[Color masterbatch](/products/color-masterbatch/) product routes. Each route
covers the material context, application questions and information a buyer
should prepare.

## Prepare an inquiry

The [Masterbatch RFQ Checklist](/resources/masterbatch-rfq-checklist/) lists the resin, process, end product, target, quantity and document details that make an inquiry easier to review.

## Understand document review

The [Technical Documents](/technical-documents/) page explains how TDS, SDS/MSDS, COA and compliance-related requests are handled. Availability depends on the exact grade, application, country and intended use.

## Common buyer questions

The [Masterbatch Buyer FAQ](/faq/) covers product range, RFQ preparation, samples, technical documents and application fit.

Product suitability and document scope remain subject to the exact grade and application review.
