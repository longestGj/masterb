Desiccant masterbatch is a functional concentrate used when moisture reaches a plastic process despite storage and drying controls. It is separate from black masterbatch: the product is selected for moisture control, not pigment or opacity.

DM2476G at a glance

DM2476G is a grey CaO desiccant masterbatch listed at 75.9% calcium oxide in a 24% recycled PE carrier. The TDS recommends a 1% starting dosage for general injection, extrusion and film blowing evaluation.

The current TDS does not replace dry resin storage or a process diagnosis. Trial the grade against a control, review recycled-carrier acceptance and request the current TDS and SDS for the exact application.
