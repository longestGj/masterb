These resources help buyers describe a masterbatch requirement in enough detail for product and document review.

## Choose a product direction

- [Black masterbatch RFQ guide](/products/black-masterbatch/) for blackness, dispersion, carrier resin and current processing issues.
- [White masterbatch RFQ guide](/products/white-masterbatch/) for whiteness, opacity, TiO2 context and document needs.
- [Color masterbatch RFQ guide](/products/color-masterbatch/) for color references, tolerance expectations and sample discussion.

## Buyer articles

Visit the [Masterbatch buyer guides](/blog/) to read selection, troubleshooting and supplier-review articles.

## Prepare an inquiry

The [Masterbatch RFQ Checklist](/resources/masterbatch-rfq-checklist/) explains the product, resin, process, target, quantity and document details to collect before contacting the team.

Use the [Request a Quote](/request-a-quote/) form when the requirement is ready for product review.

## Understand document review

The [Technical Documents](/technical-documents/) page explains how TDS, SDS/MSDS, COA and compliance-related requests are reviewed. Availability depends on the exact grade, application, country and intended use.

## Common buyer questions

The [Masterbatch Buyer FAQ](/faq/) covers product range, RFQ preparation, samples, document review and application fit.

Product suitability and document scope remain subject to the exact grade and application review.
