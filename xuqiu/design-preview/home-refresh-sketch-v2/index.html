Buyer questions

What should I send before asking for a quote?

Include the base resin, process, end product, target, current issue, known grade and document needs.

Should I choose a grade first?

No. Start with the resin, application and current requirement. A known grade helps but is not required.

How do I request TDS, SDS/MSDS or COA?

Send the request through the document form. Availability is checked by product grade, application and intended use.

Does one document cover every grade?

No. Technical and compliance-related documents apply only to the product, grade or sample they describe.

Can I discuss a sample or trial?

Sample coordination can be reviewed after the application, candidate route, trial purpose and destination are clear.

What happens after I submit an RFQ?

The request is reviewed by product route, application, document scope and the next suitable technical or commercial step.
