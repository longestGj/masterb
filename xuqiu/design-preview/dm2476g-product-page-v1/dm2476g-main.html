How a CaO desiccant masterbatch works

Calcium oxide reacts with water that reaches the melt. When wet resin, washed regrind or humid storage brings moisture into the barrel, steam can appear as bubbles, voids, splay, gels and unstable surfaces. DM2476G is an in-process drying aid for the moisture that remains after storage and pre-drying controls.

01Product and chemistry

What Is Desiccant Masterbatch DM2476G?

DM2476G is a grey pelletized desiccant masterbatch carrying 75.9% CaO in a 24% recycled PE carrier, with no CaCO₃ filler. The TDS lists general use in injection, extrusion and film blowing with a 1% recommended starting dosage.

What Is Desiccant Masterbatch?

A desiccant masterbatch is a functional concentrate selected to reduce moisture reaching the melt. DM2476G is grey because of its CaO package, not because it is intended as a pigment or appearance grade.

What Is the Difference Between Desiccant Masterbatch and Silica Gel Packets?

Silica gel protects finished packs after molding. DM2476G works in the melt during injection, extrusion or film blowing. It does not replace packaging desiccants, and packaging desiccants do not replace in-process moisture control.

How Does Calcium Oxide (CaO) Absorb Moisture in Plastic Processing?

Calcium oxide reacts with water to form calcium hydroxide. That binds free moisture which might otherwise flash into steam in the barrel. Once the CaO capacity has reacted, it is spent, which is why sealed packing and dry storage matter.

Why Is DM2476G Grey?

The TDS lists a grey granule and grey CaO at 75.9%. The appearance comes from the active desiccant package, not carbon black pigment. Natural, transparent and light-toned parts need a controlled trial because the grey pellet may shift appearance.

What Does 75.9% CaO Content Mean Commercially?

Most of each kilogram is calcium oxide, so the pellet carries substantial drying capacity at a low starting dose. Loading alone does not prove performance; compare dispersion, moisture control and the finished part on the actual line.

Why Does the TDS Show 0% CaCO₃ Filler?

DM2476G is designed as CaO plus carrier, not a limestone-padded concentrate. The zero filler value keeps more mass available for the active dryer. Price comparisons still need the actual addition rate and trial result.

What Carrier Resin Does DM2476G Use?

The carrier is 24% recycled PE. It is the polymer bridge that disperses the CaO into PE-compatible processing. The recycled content is stated on the TDS and must be accepted by the buyer and end-use program.

What Are the Processing Implications of a Recycled PE Carrier?

Recycled PE can vary more than virgin carrier in melt history and residual contamination. General PE utility work may accept it, while optical film, food-contact programs or specifications that prohibit recycled additives need review before the grade is standardized.

Is DM2476G Suitable for Food-Contact Applications?

The current TDS does not list Food Grade or a named food-contact regulation. Do not assume FDA, EU or customer approval. Request the current SDS and any available declarations for the exact grade, application and market.

02Applications and trial

What Is the Recommended Dosage for DM2476G?

The TDS lists about 1% as the recommended starting dosage. Adjust only after a controlled trial against the same material without desiccant. More concentrate is not a substitute for correcting wet storage, wet regrind or another process fault.

How Should I Run a First Trial of Desiccant Masterbatch at 1%?

Record resin, regrind share, storage history, process temperatures and the defect to remove. Run a control, dose DM2476G near 1%, compare bubbles, splay, surface and color, and retain samples of both the masterbatch and finished product.

Can I Use DM2476G for Injection Molding?

Injection molding is listed on the TDS. Moisture can cause splay, silver streaks, bubbles or weak weld areas. Trial near 1% and check dispersion, surface and grey tint while separately checking venting, temperature and fill.

Can I Use DM2476G for Film Blowing?

Film blowing is listed on the TDS. Thin film makes bubbles, gels and color shifts easy to see. Use a controlled PE film trial and compare gauge, optics, surface defects and bubble stability against the same resin without desiccant.

Can I Use DM2476G for Extrusion?

Extrusion is listed alongside injection and film blowing. Sheet, profile, pipe and compounding lines can all carry wet feedstock into the screw. Confirm the finished surface and color tolerance before fixing the dose.

When Should I Add Desiccant Masterbatch Instead of Only Pre-Drying Resin?

Pre-drying and dry storage stay first. DM2476G is considered when moisture still reaches the process through washed regrind, outdoor storage, humid plant air or an open hopper. It is a processing safety net, not permission to ignore wet handling.

How Does Moisture Cause Bubbles, Splay, and Gels in Plastic Parts?

Water turns to steam at melt temperature. The steam can expand into voids, form splay marks or disturb the surface. DM2476G targets moisture that reaches the melt; it does not remove contamination gels, trapped air or decomposition gas.

Will Desiccant Masterbatch Fix Every Bubble Problem?

No. Bubbles can also come from trapped air, degradation, incorrect temperature or poor venting. A controlled DM2476G trial is a diagnostic: a sharp reduction points to moisture, while no change requires checking the machine and resin history next.

03Packing, storage and handling

Why Is Vacuum Packaging Used for DM2476G?

CaO can consume moisture before processing. Sealed vacuum bags limit exposure during shipping and storage, protecting the drying capacity that should be available at the screw. Open only the quantity needed for the run.

How Is DM2476G Packed for Shipping and Plant Handling?

The TDS lists 5 kg vacuum PE bags, five bags in a 25 kg carton. The smaller units allow controlled opening and dosing instead of leaving one large sack exposed. Confirm pallet and container needs during quotation.

What Does Moisture Content 0.10% Max Mean on the TDS?

It is the TDS limit for moisture in the supplied masterbatch. Low incoming moisture preserves the CaO capacity for the process. If incoming QC has a suitable rapid method, use the current TDS and lot documents to define acceptance.

What Do "Dispersion: Well" and "Softening: Well" Mean for DM2476G?

These are TDS quality ratings for how the concentrate mixes and softens under normal processing. They guide selection but do not replace a line trial. Confirm that the finished film or part shows no hard agglomerates or processing instability.

What Granule Size Does DM2476G Use, and Does Size Matter for Dosing?

The TDS lists 3.0 × 3.0 × 2 mm grey granules. Verify blender feed rate during the first run if the equipment is calibrated for a different pellet geometry, because volumetric dosing depends on bulk handling behavior.

How Should I Store Desiccant Masterbatch After Delivery?

Store cartons indoors, off the floor, away from rain, steam and open water. Keep vacuum bags sealed until use and reseal opened bags tightly. Avoid long uncovered outdoor storage in humid or tropical conditions.

What Happens If I Leave an Opened Bag of DM2476G Exposed Overnight?

Ambient humidity may spend part of the CaO capacity before processing. A dose that worked from a fresh bag may then perform differently. Treat opened vacuum bags as time-sensitive and do not assume full capacity after uncontrolled exposure.

Do You Publish a Fixed Shelf Life in Months for DM2476G?

The current TDS does not state a shelf-life month count, so no fixed period is invented here. Vacuum integrity, dry storage and stock rotation protect activity. Obtain the current handling guidance and SDS with the quote and quarantine damaged bags.

Is CaO Masterbatch Hazardous to Handle, and What Documents Should I Request?

Calcium oxide reacts with water, so keep it away from water streams during handling and use dry cleanup methods. Request the current SDS for its hazard statements, PPE and first-aid guidance. This page does not invent GHS codes absent from the controlled document.

04Production practice

How Does DM2476G Compare With Simply Raising Process Temperature to "Drive Off" Moisture?

Higher temperature may change visible moisture marks, but it can also degrade polymer, create gels and waste energy without binding water. A measured CaO dose addresses the moisture itself. Keep temperature inside the resin and product window.

When Is Black Masterbatch Alone Not Enough for Wet Resin?

A black masterbatch controls carbon-black appearance and dispersion, not a dedicated moisture-scavenging function. The black grade can deliver the intended appearance while wet resin still produces bubbles. Treat appearance and drying as separate recipe functions.

Can I Blend DM2476G With Black Masterbatch in the Same Hopper?

PE-compatible recipes commonly combine low-dose concentrates, but compatibility and appearance still need a trial. Confirm each additive separately, then check the combined recipe for black dispersion, surface and total additive load.

Is DM2476G Meant to Replace Anti-Foam or Chemical Foaming Agents?

No. It is a moisture-scavenging desiccant masterbatch. Removing moisture can reduce steam bubbles, but that is not the same function as anti-foam chemistry or a chemical foaming agent. Keep the product description precise.

What Quality Checks Should Incoming QC Run on Each DM2476G Lot?

Check appearance, vacuum integrity, bag weight and moisture by an available rapid method. Match lot documents against the purchase specification and keep a sealed approved retain. Critical applications can add a plaque or short line comparison.

What Should I Tell GE CHEMICAL When Requesting a Free Sample?

Share the base resin, recycled-carrier requirements, process, regrind share, current moisture symptoms, storage conditions, planned dose and monthly volume. This lets the sample review match the actual line instead of dispatching a blind bag.

What Is a Practical Way to Compare Two Desiccant Masterbatch Quotes?

Compare cost at the addition rate needed to control the defect, not only price per kilogram. Include rejects, scrap, packing integrity, moisture control, carrier acceptance and document needs. A low-activity grade can be cheaper per bag and more expensive per good tonne.

How Does Vacuum 5 kg Packing Affect Logistics Versus One 25 kg Bag?

The carton still totals 25 kg, but contains five sealed 5 kg units. This offers better portioning and moisture protection with more bag handling than one large sack. Pallet cube, carton strength and warehouse SOP should be reviewed with the quotation.

Who Should Choose DM2476G as a Starting Desiccant Grade?

Consider DM2476G for a high-CaO, zero-filler PE-compatible trial when a recycled carrier is acceptable and a low starting dose fits the process. It is not a default choice when food-contact approval or a virgin carrier is required.

How Does Humid Climate or Outdoor Resin Storage Change Desiccant Needs?

Humid air, open storage and wet fines can introduce surface moisture even with PE. Sealed storage remains first. When moisture still reaches the hopper, DM2476G can be evaluated near the TDS starting dose instead of copying another plant’s percentage.

What Is the Role of Regrind and Washed Flake in Moisture Problems?

Regrind and washed flake have more surface area and may retain water from washing or storage. Record their share in the recipe during the trial. A change from low to high regrind can require a new dose check even with the same grade.

How Do I Know If Grey Tint From DM2476G Will Affect Product Appearance?

Run a side-by-side plaque or film at the planned dose. Dark utility products are less sensitive; natural, transparent and light-toned products may show the grey CaO package. Document a visual or instrumented appearance standard before approval.

06Purchasing and rollout

How Does GE CHEMICAL's Broader Masterbatch Capacity Help Desiccant Buyers?

The company already supplies black masterbatch with listed production scale and laboratory testing. A desiccant trial can therefore be reviewed alongside a separate black-masterbatch requirement without presenting DM2476G as a universal product.

What Is the Practical Advantage of High CaO Loading at Low Dosage?

High CaO concentration puts more drying capacity into each pellet. At the TDS starting level near 1%, that can control moisture with a smaller additive load. The final dose and color effect must still be proven on the line.

Can DM2476G Help When My Black Film Still Shows Bubbles After Switching Color Suppliers?

It can be part of the diagnosis when color is correct but moisture-like defects remain. Trial DM2476G near 1% with the current black recipe and keep sealed storage. If bubbles persist, inspect resin wetness, dryer performance and venting.

How Should Plant Operators Be Trained to Use DM2476G Day to Day?

Keep vacuum bags closed until dosing, follow the written approved ratio and reject damaged or long-open bags. Train operators that CaO reacts with water and that dry handling and SDS procedures apply. A one-page SOP prevents most field variance.

What Questions Should Purchasing Ask Before Standardizing DM2476G Across Multiple Plants?

Record each plant’s resin mix, regrind level, climate, dryers, color tolerance and recycled-carrier rules. Align one sample retain, one purchase specification and one handling SOP. Do not carry a food-contact or virgin-carrier assumption from one site to another.
