## 1. 整理结论

`公司资料.docx` 属于公司信息来源，不是具体产品技术文件。它包含公司名称、地址、主营产品、生产线数量、年产能、覆盖市场、实验室、合作品牌和可提供文件类型。

其中只有一部分内容可以在完成审核后用于网站。较强的企业声明目前没有找到对应证明，不能直接发布。另有部分产品不进入首版公开范围。

公开信息分为三层：

| 信息层级 | 当前处理 |
|---|---|
| 可保守使用 | 公司名称、主营黑/白/彩色母粒方向、按树脂和用途审核询盘、文件按牌号人工审核并邮件回复 |
| 待业务证明 | 地址、12 条生产线、5 万吨年产能、30 多个国家市场、实验室能力、合作品牌、完整文件种类 |
| 暂缓或内部使用 | 其他供应商主体、非 GE 文件、消泡母粒首发产品页、未经授权的个人联系方式、全系列认证结论 |

## 3. 英文公开文案草案

以下英文仅用于内部审阅。方括号内容必须在发布前替换、确认或删除。

### 3.1 简短公司介绍

> GE CHEMICAL & POLYMER GROUP CO., LTD reviews masterbatch inquiries for black, white and color masterbatch applications.
>
> [OPTIONAL AFTER APPROVAL: Based in Binzhou, Shandong, China, we support plastic processors, distributors and masterbatch buyers with application-focused inquiry review.]
>
> Our process starts with the buyer's base resin, processing method, end product, target color or function and document needs. Technical and compliance-related documents are reviewed by exact product grade and application, then answered by email when suitable.

### 3.2 首页公司定位

> Masterbatch Inquiries Reviewed Around Your Application
>
> GE CHEMICAL & POLYMER GROUP CO., LTD reviews black, white and color masterbatch requirements for plastic processing applications. Share your resin, process, end product, target requirement and document needs so our team can review the appropriate product scope.

### 3.3 About / Quality 页面草案

> About GE CHEMICAL & POLYMER GROUP CO., LTD
>
> GE CHEMICAL & POLYMER GROUP CO., LTD focuses on black, white and color masterbatch inquiries for plastic processing applications.
>
> We use a structured inquiry process to understand the base resin, processing method, end product, color or function target, current issue and technical document requirements. Product suitability and document availability are reviewed by exact grade and application.
>
> Technical and compliance-related documents are not available through public download. Buyers may submit a request for review, and our team will reply by email when suitable documents are available.

### 3.4 可选的公司规模段落

以下内容只能在证据和公开授权完成后启用，不应作为当前正式文案：

> [OPTIONAL AFTER EVIDENCE APPROVAL]
>
> GE CHEMICAL & POLYMER GROUP CO., LTD operates [CONFIRMED NUMBER] black masterbatch production lines with [CONFIRMED CAPACITY STATEMENT]. The company serves or has supplied customers in [CONFIRMED MARKETS].

该段落中的产能、生产线、市场和客户信息不能由现有 `公司资料.docx` 单独证明。

### 3.5 询盘流程说明

> How Your Inquiry Is Reviewed
>
> 1. Submit your product interest, base resin, processing method, end product and target requirement.
> 2. Tell us about any current quality issue, sample reference or technical document request.
> 3. Our team reviews the exact grade, application, country and document scope.
> 4. We reply by email with the next suitable step.
>
> Technical document availability depends on the exact product grade and application. Compliance-related documents, if available, apply only to specific tested grades or samples.

## 4. 中文审核版本

以下中文用于业务方判断，不作为最终中文网站文案。

> GE CHEMICAL & POLYMER GROUP CO., LTD 面向塑料加工应用，审核黑色、白色和彩色母粒询盘。
>
> [经审核后可增加：公司位于中国山东滨州，为塑料加工厂、经销商和母粒采购方提供以应用需求为核心的询盘审核服务。]
>
> 我们根据客户的基础树脂、加工方式、最终制品、目标颜色或功能以及资料需求进行审核。技术和合规相关文件按具体产品牌号及用途人工审核，适当时通过电子邮件回复。

不要直接保留的原始表述：

- “12 production lines”和“50,000 tons”在没有证明时不能作为公开事实；
- “tested and approved by more than 30 markets”含义不清，不能直接翻译；
- “well equipped lab to ensure stable quality”属于质量保证声明；
- “working with Total, Tosaf”需要客户授权并明确合作主体；
- “Available Documents: TDS · SDS · COA · COO · REACH-related documents · FDA-related”不能写成所有产品均有全部文件。

## 5. 公开安全措辞

### 可使用

- GE CHEMICAL & POLYMER GROUP CO., LTD reviews black, white and color masterbatch inquiries.
- Product and document scope is reviewed by exact grade and application.
- Share your base resin, processing method, end product and target requirement.
- Technical documents are available by request and manual review when suitable.
- Compliance-related documents, if available, apply only to specific tested grades or samples.
- Our team will reply by email after reviewing the inquiry.

### 必须获得证明后使用

- China-based masterbatch manufacturer.
- 12 production lines.
- Annual capacity over 50,000 metric tons.
- Customers in more than 30 countries.
- Well-equipped laboratory.
- Working with Total, Tosaf or other named brands.
- Full access to TDS, SDS, COA, COO, REACH or FDA documents.
- Stable quality, fast delivery, competitive price or full-series compliance.

### 禁止使用

- 所有产品均通过 FDA、RoHS、REACH 或食品接触要求；
- 所有白色母粒适合食品接触；
- 所有黑色母粒均符合 RoHS；
- 所有产品均有 TDS、SDS 或 COA；
- 网站提供 TDS、SDS、COA、FDA 或 RoHS 即时下载；
- 固定 MOQ、样品交期、价格或交期承诺；
- 未经授权公开其他公司主体名称、个人电话、邮箱、客户名称或聊天记录。

## 8. 发布结论

当前可以进入公司文案起草和页面结构设计，但尚不能把 `公司资料.docx` 全文视为已确认的公开事实。

首版公司介绍应采用保守、以询盘审核和应用核对为中心的表述。生产线、产能、市场覆盖、实验室和客户品牌等增强信任的内容，必须在业务方提供证据和公开授权后再加入。
